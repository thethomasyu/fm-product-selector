How matches are scored
Each competitor earns up to 100 points against the selected Formulated product. Higher means closer to a like-for-like comparison.
  • Function / archetype: 38 pts exact (e.g. fiber-SLU vs fiber-SLU), 18 pts same family (leveling vs leveling)
  • Wear-surface role: 16 pts (a covered underlayment is not apples-to-apples with a direct wear topping)
  • Thickness overlap: up to 12 pts (single-lift range overlap)
  • 28-day compressive: up to 12 pts (closer strength scores higher)
  • Fiber reinforcement: 9 pts match. Interior/exterior: 5 pts match
  • Cement chemistry: 8 pt bonus for same family (calcium-aluminate / Portland / gypsum / geopolymer)

On cement chemistry: a product is labelled calcium-aluminate, Portland, gypsum, or geopolymer only where the manufacturer’s TDS/SDS states it. Products that publish only “cementitious,” “hydraulic,” or a proprietary system are shown as Proprietary blend (undisclosed) and score neutral on chemistry. The tool will not assert a backbone it cannot source in either direction.

Tiers: Direct match ≥ 78. Close alternative 60 to 77. Partial overlap 40 to 59. Where a value isn’t published, that dimension scores neutral (half credit) rather than penalizing the product. Pricing is not a factor.
